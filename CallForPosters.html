Poster Session

The Poster Session offers a vibrant forum for researchers to showcase preliminary or ongoing work in the broad areas of Data and Decision Systems. Presenters will benefit from engaging conversations and constructive feedback from the reviewing committee and conference attendees. Accepted and presented posters will be submitted in IEEE Explore conference proceedings.

Based on the theme of ICDDS, the topics of interest for the poster submissions include (but are not limited to) the following:

Submission Guidelines

Manuscript Submission

Authors are encouraged to highlight the novelty and potential impact of their work. All poster submissions will undergo a double-blind peer review, so please ensure that no author names, affiliations, or identifying information are included in the manuscript.

Submission Requirements:

  • Length: 3-4 pages including all technical content, figures, tables, and references
  • Format: IEEE double-column format with 10-point font
  • File Format: PDF only
  • Style: Follow conference formatting guidelines
  • Review: Double-blind peer review process

Poster Format for Printing

You will need to bring a printed copy of your accepted poster to the poster session. Please prepare your posters in dimensions:

A0 Format

841 x 1189 mm (33.11 x 46.81 inches)

Portrait Orientation

Design Tips:

  • We will arrange for the necessary support system, including poster stands, clips, etc.
  • Posters should be visually engaging, focusing on figures, diagrams, key results, and high-level takeaways
  • Avoid large blocks of text
  • Think of your poster as a conversation starter that sparks interest and encourages feedback
